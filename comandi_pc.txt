LOG IN TEMPO REALE (equivalente di tail -f)
------------------------------------------------------------

SIANDO (da PowerShell locale):
  Get-Content "C:\Users\giand\Desktop\prog\FCGS\log\log_Siando\main.log" -Wait -Tail 30

MSI (via SSH):
  ssh giand@msi "powershell Get-Content 'C:\Users\giand\Desktop\prog\FCGS\log\log_MSI\main.log' -Wait -Tail 30"

MSIDomino12 (via SSH):
  ssh dastl@domino12 "powershell Get-Content 'C:\Users\dastl\Desktop\Giando\FCGS\log\log_MSIDomino12\main.log' -Wait -Tail 30"

MSIDomino11 (via SSH):
  ssh domin@domino11 "powershell Get-Content 'C:\Users\domin\Desktop\prog\FCGS\log\log_MSIDomino11\main.log' -Wait -Tail 30"

  ssh <user>@alienware "powershell Get-Content '<FCGS_PATH>\log\log_Alienware\main.log' -Wait -Tail 30"

------------------------------------------------------------
KILL SOLO PROCESSI FCGS (non tocca altri processi Python)
------------------------------------------------------------

Siando (locale):
  Get-WmiObject Win32_Process -Filter "Name='python.exe'" | Where-Object {$_.CommandLine -like '*federated_grid_search*'} | ForEach-Object {$children = Get-WmiObject Win32_Process -Filter "ParentProcessId=$($_.ProcessId)"; $children | ForEach-Object {Stop-Process -Id $_.ProcessId -Force -EA SilentlyContinue}; Stop-Process -Id $_.ProcessId -Force -EA SilentlyContinue}

MSI:
  ssh giand@msi "wmic process where \"Name='python.exe' and CommandLine like '%federated_grid_search%'\" delete"

MSIDomino12:
  ssh dastl@domino12 "wmic process where \"Name='python.exe' and CommandLine like '%federated_grid_search%'\" delete"

MSIDomino11:
  ssh domin@domino11 "wmic process where \"Name='python.exe' and CommandLine like '%federated_grid_search%'\" delete"

Alienware:
  ssh <user>@alienware "wmic process where \"Name='python.exe' and CommandLine like '%federated_grid_search%'\" delete"

NOTA: se wmic lascia worker orfani (processi python.exe in "Services" con tanta RAM),
killali per PID con: taskkill /F /PID <pid1> /PID <pid2> ...
