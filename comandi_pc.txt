------------------------------------------------------------
KILL SOLO PROCESSI FCGS (non tocca altri processi Python)
------------------------------------------------------------

Siando (locale):
  Get-CimInstance Win32_Process | Where-Object {$_.Name -eq 'python.exe' -and $_.CommandLine -like '*federated_grid_search*'} | ForEach-Object {Stop-Process -Id $_.ProcessId -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue}

MSI:
  ssh giand@msi 'powershell "Get-CimInstance Win32_Process | Where-Object {$_.Name -eq ''python.exe'' -and $_.CommandLine -like ''*federated_grid_search*''} | ForEach-Object {Stop-Process -Id $_.ProcessId -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue}"'

MSIDomino12:
  ssh dastl@domino12 'powershell "Get-CimInstance Win32_Process | Where-Object {$_.Name -eq ''python.exe'' -and $_.CommandLine -like ''*federated_grid_search*''} | ForEach-Object {Stop-Process -Id $_.ProcessId -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue}"'

MSIDomino11:
  ssh domin@domino11 'powershell "Get-CimInstance Win32_Process | Where-Object {$_.Name -eq ''python.exe'' -and $_.CommandLine -like ''*federated_grid_search*''} | ForEach-Object {Stop-Process -Id $_.ProcessId -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue}"'

Alienware:
  ssh daisl@alienware 'powershell "Get-CimInstance Win32_Process | Where-Object {$_.Name -eq ''python.exe'' -and $_.CommandLine -like ''*federated_grid_search*''} | ForEach-Object {Stop-Process -Id $_.ProcessId -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue}"'

geosciences — kill FCGS o stress test per PID:
